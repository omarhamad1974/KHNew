About KHIP

KHIP is an Intellectual Property law firm with fully operational offices and a network of associates and representatives throughout the Middle East. The firm is one of the finest firms practicing intellectual property and the only firm with a truly comprehensive intellectual property practice. Founded in 2004, the firm represents various clients from individual inventors to multinational companies.

We serve great innovators, helping to protect countless ideas, nurture discoveries, and bring new concepts to markets. The firm prosecuted and litigated many patents, serving corporations creating the cutting-edge technologies of the day. Khaled Hamada, the firm's founder, was for many years the acknowledged leader of the patent services of the entire country and continues to remain until a time when patents are more important than they had ever been.

Intellectual Property Value

We help our clients capture the value of their intellectual property through patent, trademark, copyright, and trade secret protection. KHIP includes experienced, globally knowledgeable lawyers in jurisdictions where your products and innovations are likely to be produced, marketed or stolen. KHIP is able to provide clients with a unique combination of national resources with personalized service.
